Business Performance Diagnosis

Identify which products, regions and discount levels drive or drain profit in a retail business, and present the results in an interactive dashboard.

PythonPandasMatplotlibSeabornPower BI
Key findings

Technology is the strongest category and Copiers is the most profitable sub-category. Furniture earns the least profit despite contributing about 32% of sales. Transactions with 0% discount produced the highest profit, while 70% discounts frequently resulted in losses.

Recommendation: Scale Technology and the West region, review pricing and costs in Furniture and the Central region, and audit high-tier discounting.

Customer Churn Analysis and Intelligence Framework

Identify customer segments and behaviors associated with subscription churn, and determine where retention efforts could be focused.

PythonSQLitePandasNumPyMatplotlibSeaborn
Key findings

Overall churn rate is 28.57% (retention rate 71.43%). Churn rates vary by plan: Basic 60.00%, Standard 22.22% and Premium 14.29%. Support escalations show a strong positive correlation (0.77) with churn.

Recommendation: Prioritize retention for Basic-plan customers through upgrade offers and onboarding, and improve escalation handling.

Credit Default Risk Analysis

Determine which borrower characteristics are associated with a higher risk of loan default using borrower and loan attributes.

PythonPandasMatplotlibSeaborn
Key findings

The overall portfolio default rate is 11.61%. The analysis compares debt-to-income ratio (DTI), interest rate and employment type individually and in combination to identify relatively lower-risk and higher-risk borrower profiles.
